# Embras Digital Experience

**Production website and content platform case study**

A portfolio overview of a production digital experience built for a Brazilian lighting manufacturer. The public website brings together a product catalog, editorial content, interactive product visualization, and a custom administration platform.

**Live website:** [embrasilumina.com.br](https://embrasilumina.com.br)

> This repository is a case study, not the application source code. The production website is publicly accessible at the link above. Its codebase, private administration, client assets, and implementation details are intentionally not distributed here.

## Project Overview

The project combines a customer-facing website with internal tools for managing products, projects, editorial content, and site content. The implementation focuses on presenting a large product range, providing interactive product experiences, and reducing repetitive publishing and content-management work.

The public website is the best place to review the finished visual experience. This README documents the scope and capabilities without reproducing client-owned content or disclosing proprietary implementation details.

## Highlights

### Motion and Interaction

- GSAP-powered entrance and scroll-triggered animations across key sections.
- Animated typography and image reveals used to guide attention through long-form pages.
- Interactive navigation, carousels, galleries, and product-detail experiences.
- Responsive interaction patterns designed for both pointer and touch input.

### Product Catalog and 3D / AR

- Searchable and filterable catalog with category navigation and product detail pages.
- Product galleries, specifications, related items, and lead-capture touchpoints.
- Interactive 3D product previews with configurable material or color variations.
- Augmented-reality placement on supported devices and browsers, allowing visitors to preview products in a real environment.

### Editorial Platform and Publishing Automation

- Blog discovery, category navigation, article pages, and related-content experiences.
- Editorial workflows for drafting, editing, scheduling, and publishing posts.
- AI-assisted support for generating article drafts and related publishing content.
- Automated publishing workflows with scheduling, duplicate avoidance, and recovery from incomplete runs.
- Editorial controls remain available so automated content can be reviewed and managed by the team.

### Custom Administration and CRUD

- Private administrative workspace for day-to-day content operations.
- Create, read, update, and delete workflows for posts, products, projects, categories, and homepage banners.
- Tables with search, filters, pagination, and status-oriented management views.
- Product data management for specifications, characteristics, categories, images, and 3D assets.
- Supporting tools for leads, users, media, automation settings, and operational records.
- Authentication and permission checks for administrative actions.

### Production Website Foundations

- Responsive page layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Search-engine metadata and structured content for public pages.
- Dynamic product, project, and editorial pages.
- Security-conscious separation between public content and administrative operations.
- Media handling for product imagery, video, and 3D experiences.

## Project Scope

The work represented by this case study includes the implementation of the public website experience, the product and editorial journeys, interactive motion and 3D/AR product features, and the custom content-management workflows that support ongoing operations.

The live site may evolve over time as the client updates content and features. This repository describes the project capabilities and is not a versioned mirror of the production website.
